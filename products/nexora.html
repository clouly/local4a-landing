Security & privacy

Your wealth never becomes someone else's data

Nexora is offline by default and has no servers of its own. There is no account to create, no analytics, and nothing to sell — because we never receive anything in the first place.

  • Fully offline core — the app makes no network requests of its own.
  • Data is stored only on your device, protected by Face ID / Touch ID and AES-GCM encryption.
  • Optional iCloud sync is end-to-end encrypted: the key lives in your iCloud Keychain, and Apple only ever stores ciphertext.
  • Document scans are encrypted on device and never leave it — they're excluded from backups and the cloud entirely.
  • AI capture runs entirely on device. No document ever reaches a server.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking SDKs.
