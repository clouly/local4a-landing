Common questions

How do I get started?

Open the app, choose your native language from the 22 available, and begin the first topic. The first topics and a daily review are free, so you can try the method before buying.

What's free, and what does Pro unlock?

The first topics and a daily review are free. Speak850 Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks the complete course, unlimited mixed practice, all example sentences, and the word-combination & grammar guides — forever, with free updates.

I bought Pro but it's not showing on my other device.

Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases", using the same Apple ID you bought with. The unlock is tied to your Apple ID at no extra cost.

Does my progress sync across devices?

Yes. When you're signed in to iCloud, your progress and settings sync through your own private iCloud. Nothing passes through the developer.

Which languages are supported?

22 native languages, including English, 中文, Español, हिन्दी, العربية, Português, Français, Deutsch, 日本語, 한국어, Русский, and more. Every word and example sentence is shown with a translation in your language.

I found a translation error or want to request a feature.

We'd love to hear it. Email us with the word or sentence and your language, and any details — it helps us improve quickly.
