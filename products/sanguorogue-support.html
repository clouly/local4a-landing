Common questions

How do I play?

Start a run and read each event, then tap a choice. Some choices are safe; others are a check — a d6 roll plus one of your attributes against a difficulty. Manage your resources and push as far as you can. When a run ends, start another — the events are drawn fresh each time.

How do the leaderboards work?

Leaderboards are optional and use Apple's Game Center. Sign in to Game Center to submit your scores and compare with others. If you don't sign in, the game stays fully offline.

What are Divine Arms?

Divine Arms are optional one-time unlocks. Each opens a distinct build style tied to a legendary lord, along with its own leaderboard — a new way to chase a high score.

I bought a Divine Arm but it's missing on my other device.

Open the store in the app and tap "Restore Purchases" using the same Apple ID you bought with. Divine Arms are non-consumable and restore at no extra cost.

Is my data private?

Yes. The game collects no personal data. Your progress is stored only on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Which languages are supported?

Twelve languages — UI and the full story — switchable in-app and following your system language.
