Three Kingdoms · Text roguelike

A nobody in a broken age. A name for the ages.

Warscribe is a single-player text roguelike. You start as a commoner in a collapsing empire — make choices through a chain of random events, roll against your wits and might, build your renown, and try to carve your name into history.

How it plays

Every choice is a roll of the dice

Each step is a story event with real decisions. Take the safe path, or gamble on a check — roll a d6 plus one of your three attributes against the difficulty. Manage your health, troops, and supplies; grow your Might, Wits, and Charm; and chase renown. One bad gamble can end the run — and every run is drawn fresh.

📜

Choice-driven events

Dozens of hand-written events across the campaign, each with branching outcomes.

🎲

Attribute checks

Roll d6 + Might / Wits / Charm against the difficulty. Fortune favors the bold — sometimes.

💀

Permadeath & many endings

Death traps, glorious victories, and endings that depend on the path you walked.

The campaign

Three acts, from rebellion to a realm split in three

Act I

The Yellow Turban Rebellion

The empire cracks. A commoner picks up a blade — or a brush — and steps into history.

Act II

Warlords Divide the Land

Alliances form and break. Choose your patrons, pick your battles, and build a name.

Act III

The Realm Split in Three

The final reckoning. Your choices across the age decide how your story ends.

Historical figures — the oath-brothers, the tyrant, the strategists — appear as cameo characters. Your hero is your own.

Play it your way

The core campaign is a complete single-player game. Divine Arms are optional one-time unlocks that open up new build styles and their own leaderboards, for players who want to chase a high score a dozen different ways. Leaderboards are optional and use Apple's Game Center.

Privacy

A single-player game that collects nothing

Warscribe is offline at its core and collects no personal data. Your progress stays on your device. The only online feature is the optional Game Center leaderboard.

  • No personal data collected, stored, or transmitted.
  • Progress (best score, campaigns, language) is stored only on your device.
  • Scores are sent to Apple's Game Center only if you sign in and submit them.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
