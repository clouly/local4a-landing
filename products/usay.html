How it works

Three steps, one tight loop

1

Listen

Hear a clear American-English model of the word or sentence, as many times as you need.

2

Shadow

Tap record and say it back. The microphone is used only while you're recording.

3

See the fix

On-device speech recognition scores each word and pinpoints the exact phoneme that tripped you up.

Privacy

Your voice stays on your phone

Usay has no accounts and no servers of its own. The whole practice loop runs on your device — and your recordings are never uploaded.

  • The microphone is used only while you're recording, to score your pronunciation.
  • Scoring prefers Apple's on-device speech recognition; recordings stay on your device and old ones are deleted automatically.
  • Practice history and stats are stored only on your device.
  • No account, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Game Center leaderboards are optional; only if you opt in are your points sent to Apple.
