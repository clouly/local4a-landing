Privacy

Your voice stays on your iPhone

No account, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. Recording and scoring run entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device speech recognition — nothing is uploaded.

  • The microphone is used only while you're recording, to score your pronunciation.
  • Recognition and scoring run on-device; your recordings stay on your phone and old ones are deleted automatically.
  • Recognition, scoring and all content work offline — a connection is only used for subscriptions and Game Center.
  • The only thing that ever leaves your device is a leaderboard total, and only if you sign in to Game Center.
