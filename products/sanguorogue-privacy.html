Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-16 · Three Kingdoms: Tides of Chaos

In one line: this is a single-player, offline game. We do not collect any personal data.

No data collection

Three Kingdoms: Tides of Chaos ("the App") is a single-player text game. We take your privacy seriously, and the App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

Offline core, optional Game Center

The App is single-player and offline at its core. Its only online feature is the optional Game Center leaderboard: only if you sign in to Game Center and submit a score is that score sent to Apple's Game Center service (handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy). If you don't use it, the App stays fully offline. Beyond this, the App communicates with no server.

On-device storage

Your game progress (such as best score, number of campaigns, and language preference) is stored only locally on your device (the system's UserDefaults) and never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the App.

No third parties

  • The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • The App does not share any information with any third party.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment details.

Children's privacy

The App collects no user information whatsoever, and therefore collects no information from children.

Changes

If a future version introduces any data-related features, we will update this policy and notify you in-app.
