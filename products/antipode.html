What's an antipode

The exact opposite point on Earth

Picture drilling straight down from where you're standing, through the very center of the planet, and out the other side. Where you'd emerge is your antipode — 12,742 km away, the full diameter of the Earth. Here's the twist: only about 4% of land has land directly opposite. Most people would surface in the middle of an ocean. Where would you?

The journey

Fall through four layers to the other side

Tap once. Antipode locates you, computes the point on the far side, and drops you into a cinematic cross-section of the planet — falling through each layer in turn until you arrive.

Layer 1

Crust

The thin rocky shell where the journey begins.

Layer 3

Layer 4

Inner core

The solid heart of the planet — then out the far side.

Your location never leaves your device

Antipode has no servers of its own and no account to create. Your location is used only on your device, to compute the point on the other side of the world.

  • Your location is used on-device to compute your antipode — it is never sent to us or stored on a server.
