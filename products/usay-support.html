Common questions

How do I practice?

Listen to the model, tap record and say it back, then read your score. Usay grades each word and shows the exact phoneme to fix. Work through the phonemes course and minimal pairs, and check your weak sounds under your progress.

Is my voice recording private?

Yes. The mic is used only while you record, recognition and scoring run entirely on your device, and recordings stay on your phone and are deleted automatically. Nothing is uploaded to us. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

Scoring or the microphone isn't working.

Check that Usay has Microphone and Speech Recognition permission in Settings → Usay. On-device recognition also needs the matching language to be available on your device. Restarting the app after granting permission usually helps.

What's free, and what does Pro add?

The daily limit counts new items only — anything you've already practised can be repeated as often as you like, on either plan. Free gives you 30 new items a day; Usay Pro doubles that to 60 and gives you a stronger position on the leaderboards. Pro is ¥12/month or ¥68/year (about ¥5.7 a month).

What happens when I hit the daily limit?

New items are locked until tomorrow, but everything you have already practised stays open. You can keep polishing it as long as you like.

How accurate is the score?

Scores come from on-device speech recognition, so treat them as a guide rather than a verdict. A recogniser can occasionally "correct" a mispronounced word back to the expected one — that is why Usay also flags unclear words and tracks your sounds over the long run. The trend matters more than any single attempt.

How do I manage or cancel Pro?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Manage or cancel anytime in the App Store app → your account → Subscriptions. If Pro isn't showing on a new device, tap "Restore Purchase" with the same Apple ID.

Which native languages are supported?

Chinese, Japanese and Korean. The interface, translations and — importantly — the pronunciation guidance all change, because the sounds each group struggles with are different. The pronunciation taught is American English.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Recognition, scoring and all content work offline. A connection is only used for subscriptions and Game Center rankings.

Where does the pronunciation data come from?

From the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (Carnegie Mellon University), a widely used open pronunciation resource. It's bundled in the app; there's an in-app licenses page with the details.
