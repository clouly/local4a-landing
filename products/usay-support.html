Common questions

How do I practice?

Listen to the model, tap record and say it back, then read your score. Usay grades each word and shows the exact phoneme to fix. Work through the phonemes course and minimal pairs, and check your weak sounds under your progress.

Is my voice recording private?

Yes. The mic is used only while you record, scoring prefers Apple's on-device speech recognition, and recordings stay on your device and are deleted automatically. Nothing is uploaded to us. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

Scoring or the microphone isn't working.

Check that Usay has Microphone and Speech Recognition permission in Settings → Usay. On-device recognition also needs the matching language to be available on your device. Restarting the app after granting permission usually helps.

What's free, and what does Pro add?

Free gives you 30 new items to learn each day, with unlimited replays of anything you've already practiced; the allowance refreshes the next day. Usay Pro doubles that to 60 new items a day and gives you an edge on the leaderboards. It's a monthly or yearly subscription — the price is shown in the App Store.

How do I manage or cancel Pro?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Manage or cancel anytime in the App Store app → your account → Subscriptions. If Pro isn't showing on a new device, tap "Restore Purchase" with the same Apple ID.

Which languages does the app support?

The interface is available in English, 简体中文, 日本語 and 한국어. The pronunciation it teaches is American English.

Where does the pronunciation data come from?

From the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (Carnegie Mellon University), a widely used open pronunciation resource. It's bundled in the app; there's an in-app licenses page with the details.
