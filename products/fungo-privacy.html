隐私政策

生效日期:2026 年 8 月 19 日 · 最后更新:2026 年 8 月 19 日

简单说:我们不收集你的任何个人数据。

四象棋灵没有自己的服务器,不含任何广告、分析或追踪工具,也不会把你的信息发送给我们或任何第三方。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

没有。本 App 的开发者不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人身份信息。具体而言,本 App:

  • 不含任何第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK;
  • 不含开发者自建的服务器或后端,不会向我们发送任何数据;
  • 不请求访问你的通讯录、位置、相册、摄像头或麦克风;
  • 不使用 Cookie 或跨 App 追踪标识符(不进行 App 跟踪透明度 / ATT 意义上的追踪)。

2. 存储在你设备上的数据

为了让游戏正常运行,以下设置仅保存在你的设备本地,不会上传:

数据用途
段位积分记录你的休闲段位进度
棋盘皮肤选择记住你选用的棋盘
音乐静音开关记住你的音效偏好

这些数据存放在系统的应用偏好设置中。若你开启了 iCloud 备份,它们可能随设备备份一并保存(由 Apple 管理)。删除本 App 即会删除这些本地数据。

3. Apple 提供的服务

本 App 使用了 Apple 的两项系统服务。这些服务由 Apple 运营,并适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

Game Center(游戏中心)

  • 如果你登录了 Game Center,Apple 会向本 App 提供你的公开昵称(display name),用于在对局界面显示对战双方。
  • 你的段位积分会提交到 Game Center 排行榜,成就进度会提交到 Game Center。
  • 进行实时联机对局时,你的落子与技能操作会通过 Apple 的 Game Center 服务转发给对手。这些数据仅用于进行当前对局,我们不会记录或留存。
  • Game Center 是可选的。不登录也可以正常游玩(将与 AI 对战)。

App 内购买(StoreKit)

  • 所有购买均由 Apple 处理。我们不会接触到你的支付方式、银行卡号或账单信息。
  • 本 App 仅向系统查询"某项内容是否已购买",以决定是否解锁对应角色或棋盘。

4. 数据的共享与出售

我们不出售、不出租、不交易、也不以任何方式共享你的个人数据 —— 因为我们本就不持有任何此类数据。

5. 儿童隐私

本 App 不会有意收集任何人(包括儿童)的个人信息。由于我们不收集任何数据,也就不存在针对儿童的数据收集行为。Game Center 相关功能由 Apple 提供,家长可通过设备的"屏幕使用时间"进行管理。

6. 你的权利

由于我们不持有你的任何个人数据,通常意义上的访问、更正或删除请求无需向我们提出。你可以:

  • 删除本 App —— 即清除全部本地数据;
  • 通过 iOS 系统设置管理 Game Center 账户与数据;
  • 通过 Apple 管理购买记录与退款申请。

7. 本政策的变更

若未来版本新增了涉及数据的功能,我们会更新本页面并修改顶部的"最后更新"日期。重大变更将在 App 内说明。

Privacy Policy

Effective: 19 August 2026 · Last updated: 19 August 2026

In short: we collect no personal data.

GO SPIRITS has no servers of its own and contains no ads, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

1. Information we collect

None. The developer of this app does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. Specifically, this app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs; has no developer-operated server or backend; does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, camera, or microphone; and uses no cookies or cross-app tracking identifiers (no tracking in the App Tracking Transparency sense).

2. Data stored on your device

The following settings are saved locally on your device only and are never uploaded: your rank points, your selected board skin, and your music mute preference. They are stored in the system's app preferences and may be included in an iCloud backup if you have one enabled (managed by Apple). Deleting the app deletes this local data.

3. Apple services used

This app uses two Apple system services, operated by Apple and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Game Center. If you are signed in, Apple provides your public display name, which is shown in match screens. Your rank points are submitted to a Game Center leaderboard and achievement progress is reported to Game Center. During real-time online matches, your moves are relayed to your opponent through Apple's Game Center service; we do not log or retain them. Game Center is optional — you can play without signing in (against AI).

In-App Purchases (StoreKit). All purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment method, card number, or billing information. The app only asks the system whether a given item has been purchased, in order to unlock the corresponding character or board.

4. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data — we do not hold any.

5. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because we collect no data at all, no child-directed data collection occurs. Game Center features are provided by Apple and can be managed by parents via Screen Time.

6. Your rights

Because we hold none of your personal data, access, correction, and deletion requests generally need not be directed to us. You may delete the app to remove all local data, manage your Game Center account and data in iOS Settings, and manage purchases or request refunds through Apple.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that involve data, we will update this page and revise the "last updated" date above. Material changes will be explained in the app.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: hello.local4a@gmail.com
