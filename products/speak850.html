The method

A small core that says almost anything

In 1930, linguist C. K. Ogden chose 850 words that, used well, can express almost any everyday idea. Master this core and you can communicate right away — then grow naturally toward full English. Speak850 turns that method into a guided, modern course you do a little of each day.
