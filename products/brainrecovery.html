For life after stroke

A few gentle minutes of practice each day

Brain Recovery is a cognitive and speech training aid for people recovering after a stroke. Big text, big buttons, simple steps, and an encouraging tone — something to do together with family.

Cognitive training

Practice attention, memory, and number sense

🔢

Schulte Table

Tap the numbers in order to train attention and visual scanning.

🃏

Memory Match

Flip cards and match the pairs to train memory.

➕

Calculation

Simple add, subtract, and multiply to keep number sense sharp.

🔁

Sequence Memory

Recall a number sequence and tap it back to train working memory.

Speech training

Practice speaking and understanding

🖼️

Name the Picture

Say what you see. On-device speech recognition can check your pronunciation, or you can self-assess.

👂

Listen & Choose

Hear a word and tap the matching picture to practice comprehension.

Please note. Brain Recovery is a rehabilitation training aid. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment, and it does not replace professional medical or rehabilitation care. Please follow your doctor's or therapist's guidance, and seek medical care if you feel unwell.
Privacy

Your data stays on your device

Brain Recovery keeps all training data on your own device. No servers, no personal information, no ads, no third-party tracking.

  • Training records and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The microphone is used only during "Name the Picture" — audio is never stored or uploaded.
  • No account, no analytics, no advertising SDKs.
  • Clear your data anytime in Settings, or by deleting the app.
