Break it down, then speak it.

How it works

Four steps, one unbroken chain

1

See the structure

A 40-word sentence splits into thought groups, each labelled with its role. One tap strips it to the bare skeleton.

2

Hear it chunked

The model reading pauses between thought groups — the same boundaries you just saw, so the structure becomes a rhythm.

3

Read it out loud

Build the sentence from the end, one thought group at a time, until the whole thing flows. Tap record and say it back — recording stops by itself.

4

See what to fix

Scores for accuracy, completeness and fluency — with every word colour-coded and the problem traced to the exact phoneme.

Privacy

Your voice stays on your iPhone

No account, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. Recording and scoring run entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device speech recognition — nothing is uploaded.

  • The microphone is used only while you're recording, to score your pronunciation.
  • Recognition and scoring run on-device; your recordings stay on your phone and old ones are deleted automatically.
  • Recognition, scoring and all content work offline — a connection is used only to make or restore the purchase.
  • No account, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Nothing is uploaded — your voice never leaves the device.
