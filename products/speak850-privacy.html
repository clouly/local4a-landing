Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: June 14, 2026

Speak850 is an app for learning Basic English (C. K. Ogden's method). The short version: the app collects nothing and works on your own device.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

Nothing. Speak850 does not collect, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Where your data lives

Your learning progress and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also synced through your own private iCloud (provided by Apple). The developer has no access to this data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive or store your payment information.

Children

The app is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
