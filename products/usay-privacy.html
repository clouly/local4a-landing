Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-29 · Usay

In one line: Usay is designed to work entirely on your device. We collect nothing — no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs.

Data we collect

None. Usay has no account system, no sign-in, and no servers of its own. We do not collect, receive, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is nothing for us to sell or share, because nothing ever reaches us.

Microphone & speech recognition

Usay records your voice only when you tap the record button, solely to evaluate your pronunciation. In the current version, recognition and scoring run entirely on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your recordings are stored only on your device, older recordings are deleted automatically, and nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

A future version may add optional cloud-based recognition for greater accuracy. If that happens, it will be clearly indicated in the app, you will be able to choose whether to use it, and this policy will be updated beforehand to describe exactly what is sent and to whom.

Practice data

Your practice history, scores, statistics and settings are stored only on your device (using the system's local storage) and never leave it, except as described below for Game Center. Removing the app removes this data.

Game Center (optional)

If you choose to sign in to Apple's Game Center, your practice point totals are submitted to Apple to display leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy, and we never receive this data. You can use the entire app without Game Center.

Subscriptions

Usay Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store any payment information — we only learn whether a subscription is active.

No third parties

  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • Pronunciation data is based on the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (Carnegie Mellon University), bundled with the app; using it involves no network request.

Children

Usay collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
