System requirements

  • Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later)
  • macOS 14 or later
  • 8 GB RAM minimum — 16 GB recommended for 7B models
  • Internet connection for the first model download (offline after that)

Common questions

How do I connect my tool to LocalGateAI?

Copy the Base URL and API Key shown in LocalGateAI, then paste them into any app that supports a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint (Cursor, Cherry Studio, Dify, and more).

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally on your Mac — the gateway is localhost-only and there's no telemetry. See our Privacy Policy for details.

A model won't download or runs slowly. What can I do?

Make sure you have a stable internet connection for the first download, enough free disk space for the model, and enough free RAM. Smaller models run better on 8 GB Macs.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email us with as much detail as you can — your macOS version, Mac model, and what happened. It helps us reproduce and fix issues faster.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit the app and move it to the Trash. Downloaded models and settings are stored locally and are removed with the app's data.
