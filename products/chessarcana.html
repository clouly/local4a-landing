How it plays

Serious chess underneath, magic on top

A pure rules engine handles legal moves, castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate, stalemate, the fifty-move rule, threefold repetition, and insufficient material — wins are decided by checkmate. On top sits an optional fantasy skill layer, and every skill move still obeys the real rules: you can never leave your own king in check.

♟️

Complete FIDE rules

A tested, pure-function rules engine — the same correct chess across every mode.

Three timed modes

Fair by design

The four hero packs are separate one-time purchases at different prices. They unlock new skills and flavor — never a base stat boost. Pieces keep their standard values, and every skill still plays by the real rules. You can win the whole game with the free hero.

Privacy

No accounts of ours. No tracking.

Chess Arcana has no servers of its own. Your profile and stats stay on your device, online play runs through Apple's Game Center, and there's no third-party tracking.

  • Your rating, records, and settings are stored on your device.
  • Online matches use Apple's Game Center, subject to Apple's privacy policy.
  • No third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.
