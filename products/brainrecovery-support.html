Common questions

How do I get started?

Open the app, choose your language, and start with today's plan. Exercises are short — a few minutes a day is enough to build a habit. There's no account to create.

What's the difference between the patient and family views?

The patient view focuses on simple, encouraging exercises. The family view shows progress charts, training mix, and history so family can follow along. Switch between them with one tap.

Is my data private?

Yes. All training data stays on your device — nothing is uploaded to a server. The microphone is used only during the speech exercise, and audio is never stored or uploaded. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I clear my data?

Go to Settings → Clear training data inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

Which languages are supported?

12 languages, switchable anytime in the app's settings.

What is "Support us" in the settings?

It's a fully optional tip to support development, handled by Apple's in-app purchase. It's voluntary and does not unlock any features — the app's exercises are the same with or without it.

Brain Recovery is a rehabilitation training aid. It is not a medical device and does not replace professional medical diagnosis or therapy. Please follow your doctor's or therapist's guidance, and seek medical care if you feel unwell.
