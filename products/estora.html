Security & privacy

Your family's wealth is not our business model

Estora has no accounts and no servers of its own. It's offline by default, and the only two things it ever fetches contain none of your data.

  • Your data is stored on your device, AES-GCM encrypted and locked behind Face ID / Touch ID.
  • Public exchange rates are the only routine network request — rate data comes in, none of your data goes out.
  • iCloud sync is optional and end-to-end encrypted: the key lives in your iCloud Keychain, and Apple only ever stores ciphertext.
  • Document scans stay on the device that created them — excluded from sync and backups entirely.
  • AI capture runs entirely on device. No document ever reaches a server.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking SDKs.
