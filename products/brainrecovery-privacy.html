Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-22 · Brain Recovery

In one line: Brain Recovery keeps all your training data on your own device. We do not upload it to any server, do not collect personal information, and include no ads or third-party tracking.

Data we process

  • Training records (scores, durations, dates, exercise types): stored only in the app's on-device database, used to show progress and streaks.
  • Preferences (role, interface language): stored only on your device.
  • Microphone & speech recognition: used only during the "Name the Picture" exercise, when you tap "Speak", to check pronunciation. Recognition runs on-device where supported; audio is never stored and never uploaded.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect names, phone numbers, emails, location, or other personally identifying information.
  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • We never sell or share your data.

Storage & deletion

Your data lives on your device. You can erase it anytime via Settings → Clear training data, or by deleting the app.

Medical disclaimer

This app is a training aid for cognitive and speech rehabilitation. It is not a medical device, does not provide diagnosis or treatment, and does not replace professional medical or rehabilitation care. Please follow your doctor's or therapist's guidance.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
